Privacy and accuracy

Spam Spotter checks text and links in this browser. It does not send the message or link you enter to a server.

Your text-size choice and scam report notes are saved in this browser on this device. Reports stay here until you clear them or clear this browser's site data. Do not include names, account numbers, passwords, or other private details.

This prototype uses a short list of common warning signs. It does not check a live scam database and cannot prove that a message or website is safe. When unsure, do not click, reply, or pay. Contact the organization using a number or website you already trust.

When in doubt, pause. A result cannot confirm that a message or website is genuine. Contact the organization using a phone number on your card or an official address you type yourself.
